What I build

Three moves, on repeat

  • Logs in. Credentials stay on your machine; the agent handles 2FA and session quirks you'd rather not.
  • Navigates. Reads a page the way you do — buttons, tables, menus — not by brittle selectors.
  • Completes. Runs the whole job end to end and reports back what happened.

How it works

From brief to done in three steps

  1. I

    You describe the job

    "Every morning, pull reports from X and file them in Y." One paragraph is enough.

  2. II

    The agent learns the site

    It explores the target once, maps the flow, and shows you exactly what it will do before touching anything.

  3. III

    It runs, you review

    Unattended runs with a log of every action. You see receipts, not promises.
